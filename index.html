Cambridge, MA, USA 02139
Schwarzman College of Computing, MIT

Hi all! I am a 2nd-year PhD student at MIT IDSS and CSAIL, advised by Prof. Dylan Hadfield-Menell. My research was generously supported by the MIT Michael Hammer Fellowship.

Previously, I obtained my Master’s degree from Harvard University, where I worked with Prof. Hima Lakkaraju and my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from TUM, with a minor in Computational Linguistics at LMU, supervised by Prof. Hinrich Schütze. Also, I had the opportunity to visit the Stanford NLP Group, collaborating with Prof. Diyi Yang for one year.

My research interests mainly lie in scalable supervision and scalable oversight, i.e., how to train and oversee superhuman-level AI systems.
